# Report 4 — Seller Risk Scorecard

## 1. Stakeholder

**Primary Stakeholder:** Company Leadership / Seller Operations

Leadership needs a unified view of seller performance to identify sellers who generate significant revenue but may also create operational or customer-experience risks.

---

## 2. Business Problem

Leadership currently does not have a single view combining seller revenue, order volume, delivery performance, and customer satisfaction.

A seller may generate high revenue while also having:

* Poor customer review scores
* High late-delivery rates
* A delivery performance rate worse than the overall platform

The report combines these metrics into a single seller-level scorecard and flags high-revenue sellers that also demonstrate potential performance risk.

---

## 3. Report Requirement

The report provides the following metrics for each seller:

* Total orders fulfilled
* Total revenue generated
* Average customer review score
* Late delivery percentage
* Platform average late-delivery percentage
* Revenue rank
* Risk flag

A seller is classified as **High Risk** when:

1. The seller belongs to the top 20% revenue bucket, **and**
2. The seller is either:

   * In the bottom 20% review-score bucket, **or**
   * Has a late-delivery rate above the platform average.

All other sellers are classified as **OK**.

---

## 4. Business Use

This scorecard gives leadership a consolidated view of seller performance.

It can be used to:

* Identify high-revenue sellers requiring closer operational review.
* Compare seller revenue with customer satisfaction.
* Identify sellers with delivery performance above the platform late-delivery rate.
* Distinguish high-volume sellers from high-risk high-revenue sellers.
* Support seller performance monitoring and operational decisions.

The report does not evaluate sellers using revenue alone. It combines financial performance with customer and delivery indicators.

---

# 6. CTE Pipeline

The report uses six CTEs to independently calculate seller revenue, delivery performance, customer satisfaction, the platform benchmark, and seller risk buckets.

---

## 6.1 `seller_orders_revenue`

### Purpose

Calculates the order volume and total revenue generated by each seller.

### Logic

The CTE groups `order_items` by `seller_id`.

```sql
count(distinct oi.order_id)
```

calculates the number of unique orders fulfilled by each seller.

```sql
sum(oi.price)
```

calculates the seller's total product revenue.

### What It Does

Each seller receives:

* `orders_fulfilled`
* `total_revenue`

### Why It Matters

These metrics establish the seller's business scale and revenue contribution to the marketplace.

### Output

```text
seller_id
orders_fulfilled
total_revenue
```

---

## 6.2 `seller_late`

### Purpose

Calculates the percentage of seller-associated order-item records that were delivered after the estimated delivery date.

### Logic

The CTE joins `order_items` with `orders1` using `order_id`.

A delivery is considered late when:

```sql
order_delivered_customer_date > order_estimated_delivery_date
```

The late percentage is calculated as:

```sql
100.0 * late_orders / delivered_records
```

The result is rounded to one decimal place.

### What It Does

Each seller receives a `late_pct` representing the percentage of delivered seller-associated records that were late.

### Why It Matters

This identifies sellers associated with higher levels of delivery delay.

### Output

```text
seller_id
late_pct
```

> **Important:** Because the calculation starts from `order_items`, the denominator is based on seller-associated order-item records rather than `COUNT(DISTINCT order_id)`.

---

## 6.3 `seller_reviews`

### Purpose

Calculates the average customer review score associated with each seller.

### Logic

The CTE joins:

```text
order_items
      ↓
order_reviews
      ↓
orders1
```

Only orders with a non-null delivery date are included.

The average review score is calculated using:

```sql
avg(ors.review_score)
```

and rounded to two decimal places.

### What It Does

Each seller receives an average customer review score.

### Why It Matters

Revenue alone does not show customer experience. The review score provides a customer-satisfaction indicator for each seller.

### Output

```text
seller_id
avg_review
```

---

## 6.4 `platform_avg_late`

### Purpose

Calculates the overall platform late-delivery percentage.

### Logic

The calculation uses all delivered orders in `orders1`.

A delivery is considered late when:

```sql
order_delivered_customer_date > order_estimated_delivery_date
```

The percentage is calculated across delivered orders.

### What It Does

Creates a platform-wide benchmark:

```text
platform_late_pct
```

### Why It Matters

A seller's late-delivery rate can then be compared against the overall marketplace performance rather than being evaluated in isolation.

### Output

```text
platform_late_pct
```

---

## 6.5 `seller_combined`

### Purpose

Combines the seller-level revenue, order volume, delivery performance, and review metrics into one dataset.

### Logic

The CTE starts with `seller_orders_revenue` and uses `LEFT JOIN` to attach:

* `seller_late`
* `seller_reviews`

using `seller_id`.

### What It Does

Creates one seller-level record containing the major performance metrics.

### Why It Matters

This creates the foundation for the seller risk classification.

### Output

```text
seller_id
orders_fulfilled
total_revenue
late_pct
avg_review
```

---

## 6.6 `seller_ranked`

### Purpose

Places sellers into revenue and review-performance buckets.

### Logic

Two `NTILE(5)` window functions are used.

### Revenue Bucket

```sql
ntile(5) over (
    order by sc.total_revenue desc
)
```

Divides sellers into five revenue groups.

Therefore:

```text
revenue_bucket = 1
```

represents the highest revenue bucket, approximately the top 20% of sellers.

### Review Bucket

```sql
ntile(5) over (
    order by sc.avg_review asc nulls first
)
```

Divides sellers into five review-score groups.

Because the ordering is ascending:

```text
review_bucket = 1
```

represents the lowest review-score bucket, approximately the bottom 20%.

`NULLS FIRST` places sellers without an available review score at the beginning of the ordering.

### Why It Matters

This allows the report to identify sellers that combine high revenue with comparatively poor customer satisfaction.

### Output

```text
seller_id
orders_fulfilled
total_revenue
late_pct
avg_review
revenue_bucket
review_bucket
```

---

# 7. Final Query Logic

The final query combines the ranked seller data with the platform-wide late-delivery benchmark.

A `CROSS JOIN` is used because `platform_avg_late` contains one platform-level value that applies to every seller.

### Revenue Rank

```sql
rank() over (
    order by sr.total_revenue desc
)
```

assigns each seller a revenue ranking.

### Risk Classification

The report applies the following business rule:

```sql
case
    when sr.revenue_bucket = 1
         and (
              sr.review_bucket = 1
              or sr.late_pct > pal.platform_late_pct
         )
    then 'High Risk'
    else 'OK'
end
```

In simple terms:

```text
Top 20% Revenue
       │
       ├── Bottom 20% Review Score ──► High Risk
       │
       └── Late Rate > Platform Average ──► High Risk
```

A seller must first be in the highest revenue bucket. The seller is then flagged if either customer satisfaction is in the lowest bucket or late deliveries exceed the platform benchmark.

# 9. Business Interpretation

The report provides leadership with a unified seller-level view combining:

**Revenue + Order Volume + Customer Satisfaction + Delivery Performance**

This makes it possible to distinguish between sellers that generate substantial revenue and maintain strong customer experience indicators, and high-revenue sellers that also exhibit potential delivery or satisfaction risks.

### Key analytical signals

**High Revenue + Good Review + Acceptable Delivery**

Indicates a seller generating substantial business volume without triggering the defined risk conditions.

**High Revenue + Low Review**

Identifies sellers generating significant revenue while belonging to the lowest review-performance bucket.

**High Revenue + Late Delivery Above Platform Average**

Identifies sellers whose delivery performance is worse than the overall platform benchmark.

**High Revenue + Low Review + High Late Delivery**

Represents a seller triggering multiple risk conditions simultaneously.

The `High Risk` flag is therefore a business-rule indicator for **operational review**, rather than a conclusion that a seller should automatically be removed from the platform.

# 10. SQL Concepts Used

| SQL Concept       | Application                                                     |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| CTEs              | Break the seller analysis into logical stages                   |
| `COUNT(DISTINCT)` | Calculate unique fulfilled orders                               |
| `SUM()`           | Calculate seller revenue                                        |
| `AVG()`           | Calculate review and delivery metrics                           |
| `CASE`            | Identify late deliveries and assign risk flags                  |
| `JOIN`            | Combine seller, order, and review data                          |
| `LEFT JOIN`       | Preserve seller records when supporting metrics are unavailable |
| `CROSS JOIN`      | Attach the single platform benchmark to every seller            |
| `RANK()`          | Rank sellers by revenue                                         |
| `NTILE(5)`        | Divide sellers into five performance buckets                    |
| Window Functions  | Calculate rankings and performance groups                       |
| `NULLS FIRST`     | Control ordering of sellers without review scores               |
| Aggregation       | Produce seller-level performance metrics                        |
